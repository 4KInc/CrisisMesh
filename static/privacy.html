CrisisMesh

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

CrisisMesh is an emergency-coordination service operated by [[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]] ("we", "us"). Schools, nonprofits, and other resource-constrained organizations use CrisisMesh to coordinate their internal response during a fire, severe-weather, medical, or active-threat incident. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Mobile information and SMS consent. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

1. Information we collect

  • Roster information supplied by your organization: name, role, building/zone assignment, and any mobility-assistance need recorded so responders can prioritize help.
  • Mobile phone number, which you provide yourself through our SMS opt-in form, or which your organization records for you with your permission.
  • Consent records: the exact consent language you agreed to, the date and time of your opt-in, the IP address and browser used to submit it, and the date of any later opt-out. We keep these to demonstrate compliance with messaging regulations.
  • Message content you send to CrisisMesh: incident reports and safety check-in replies (SAFE, SOS, INJURED, EVACUATED), along with the time received.
  • Operational logs: an append-only audit record of each automated action taken during an incident.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver emergency coordination and safety check-in text messages you have consented to receive.
  • To track who is safe, injured, evacuated, or unaccounted for during an active incident, and to surface that status to your organization's incident commander.
  • To produce an after-incident report and audit trail for your organization.
  • To respond to your HELP requests and support enquiries.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

3. SMS messaging program

CrisisMesh sends emergency coordination and safety check-in messages only to mobile numbers with a recorded opt-in. Message frequency varies by incident. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for assistance. Full program details are in our SMS Terms and Conditions.

4. Who we share information with

We share personal information only in these circumstances:

  • Your organization. Incident status and check-in replies are visible to the incident commander and safety staff at the organization that enrolled you.
  • Service providers that operate the platform on our behalf, under contract and only for that purpose: Twilio (SMS delivery), Meta (WhatsApp delivery), Slack (workspace messaging), and Google Cloud (hosting and the Gemini models that generate situation reports).
  • Emergency services and legal obligations. We may disclose information to 911 dispatchers or other authorities when needed to respond to a life-threatening emergency, or where required by law.

We never share mobile information or SMS consent data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Retention

Incident records and check-in history are retained for the period your organization configures, and consent records are retained for at least four years after opt-out, as messaging regulations require. You may ask us to delete information that we are not legally required to keep.

6. Security

Inbound webhooks are cryptographically signature-verified, message content is screened for prompt-injection and personal-data leakage before it reaches any model, and every automated action is written to an append-only audit log.

7. Children

CrisisMesh is provided to organizations for use by staff and adult responders. We do not knowingly enroll a mobile number belonging to a child under 13 in the SMS program.

8. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any CrisisMesh message to stop all SMS immediately.
  • Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.
  • Ask your organization's administrator to remove you from the roster.

9. Safety notice

CrisisMesh coordinates an organization's internal response alongside 911 and qualified responders. It never replaces them. In an emergency, call 911 first.
