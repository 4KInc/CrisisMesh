1. Program description

CrisisMesh sends emergency coordination and safety check-in text messages to staff and designated responders at the school, nonprofit, or organization that enrolled you. Messages are conversational and transactional. They include:

  • Acknowledgement of an incident report you send in.
  • A situation report for an active incident at your site — classification, evacuation routes, assembly point, and accountability status.
  • A request to confirm your safety status during an active incident.
  • Confirmation that your check-in reply was recorded.

This program sends no marketing, promotional, or advertising messages of any kind.

2. How to opt in

There are two ways to join, and both require your express consent:

  • Web form. Complete the CrisisMesh SMS opt-in form, entering your name, organization, and mobile number, and tick the consent checkbox — which is unchecked by default and must be actively selected. We then text you a confirmation message; reply YES to complete enrollment.
  • Text to join. Text START to the CrisisMesh number provided by your organization. Your inbound message is itself your consent, and we reply with a confirmation.

Consent is not a condition of employment, enrollment, or any purchase. You will not receive messages until enrollment is complete.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. CrisisMesh is event-driven: during an active incident you may receive several messages within a short period; in a month with no incident and no drill you may receive none.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. CrisisMesh charges you nothing for these messages; charges depend on your mobile plan and carrier.

Because HELP is reserved by the mobile carriers for program information, the emergency "I need assistance" check-in keyword is SOS — replying HELP during an incident returns program info and does not flag you as needing rescue. In a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

7. Not a substitute for 911

CrisisMesh coordinates your organization's internal response alongside 911 and qualified responders — it never replaces them. Never rely on a text message to summon emergency services. If you are in danger or someone is hurt, call 911 first. CrisisMesh does not monitor messages in real time and provides no medical, tactical, or evacuation instruction beyond the playbooks your own organization has approved.
