CrisisMesh

Emergency Alert SMS Sign-Up

For staff and designated responders at organizations using CrisisMesh.

CrisisMesh is an emergency coordination service operated by Blockintel Inc. Schools, nonprofits, and other organizations use it to coordinate their internal response during a fire, severe-weather, medical, or active-threat incident — tracking who is safe, who needs help, and which routes are clear.

Sign up below to receive emergency coordination and safety check-in text messages during an incident at your site, and to be able to check in by replying SAFE, SOS, INJURED, or EVACUATED.

What happens next

  1. We text a confirmation message to the number you entered.
  2. Reply YES to that message to complete your enrollment. You will not receive alerts until you do.
  3. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for support information.

CrisisMesh never replaces 911. It coordinates your organization's internal response alongside emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, call 911 first.
